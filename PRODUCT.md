## Users

1. **Residential Villa & Homeowners in Bali**: Local residents, expatriates, and long-term villa tenants requiring urgent AC repairs, deep cleaning, or freon charging without complex bookings or uncertain estimates.
2. **Hospitality & Villa Property Managers**: Boutique hotels, private luxury villas, Airbnb hosts, and cafes in Canggu, Seminyak, Kuta, Ubud, and Sanur needing guaranteed 60-minute emergency dispatch, clean indoor standard (tarps & clean slippers), and signed official tax invoices for property accounting.
3. **Expatriates & International Tourists**: English-speaking guests who require clear bilingual communication, transparent fixed rates upfront, and quick WhatsApp dispatch.

## Product Purpose

Jawara Teknik Servis AC Bali provides transparent, rapid-response on-call air conditioning maintenance, repair, and installation services across Bali. The website exists to turn emergency HVAC distress (leaking water, warm airflow, foul odors, electrical faults) into immediate, frictionless WhatsApp bookings backed by a 30-day work warranty.

## Positioning

Unlike unverified marketplace listings, brokers, or technicians with hidden diagnostic surcharges, Jawara Teknik operates a physically registered, Google Maps-verified workshop with transparent upfront pricing, multi-brand certified technicians (Inverter specialists for Daikin, Panasonic, Sharp, Gree, LG), and an unconditional 30-day warranty on all repairs and parts.

## Operating Context

- **Urgent Emergency Context**: Visitors frequently arrive with actively dripping air conditioners or hot rooms during Bali's humid climate. Decisions are made quickly; reassurance, response time (within 60 minutes), and pricing clarity must be instant.
- **Bilingual Environment**: Both Indonesian homeowners and foreign villa managers/tourists book technicians daily.
- **WhatsApp-Centric Dispatch**: Booking happens via pre-filled structured WhatsApp messages that eliminate back-and-forth text exchanges.
